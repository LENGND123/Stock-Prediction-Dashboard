Forecasting Disclaimer & Information

The AI recommendations are calculated based on the percent difference between the current Close price and tomorrow's forecasted price:

  • Strong Buy: Forecasted increase is greater than 1.5%
  • Buy: Forecasted increase is between 0.2% and 1.5%
  • Hold: Forecasted change is within ±0.2%
  • Sell: Forecasted decrease is between -0.2% and -1.5%
  • Strong Sell: Forecasted decrease is greater than -1.5%

* Backtest results (MAE, RMSE, R²) represent performance calculated on a temporal 20% validation split. Past performance does not guarantee future results. This tool is purely educational and does not constitute financial advice.
